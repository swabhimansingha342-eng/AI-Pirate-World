## Workflow

1. Pull the latest develop branch.
2. Switch to your feature branch.
3. Make your changes.
4. Test in Unity.
5. Commit your changes.
6. Push your branch.
7. Create a Pull Request.
8. Team reviews the changes.
9. Merge into develop.

## Unity Rules

Do not modify another person's system
without discussing it first.

Do not rename shared assets without informing
the team.

Do not delete scripts created by another team member.

Avoid simultaneous editing of the same Unity scene.

Use prefabs and modular components whenever possible.
